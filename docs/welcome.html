Why Trove

Everything a sale needs, and nothing taken from it.

Trove handles payment, pickup and delivery in one place, so a sale between two people feels as safe as buying from a shop.

Money held until delivery

When a buyer pays by card, Trove holds the money. It goes to the seller once the buyer has the item.

  • Buyers get a refund if an item never ships
  • Sellers see the money is there before they post

Meet up with a code

For local pickups, the buyer gets a 6-digit code. The seller enters it at the handoff, and the sale is done.

  • Pay by card ahead, or pay in person
  • Suggested public meeting spots

Post from your phone

Take photos, add a price and a few details, and the listing is live. Add a whole box of items at once as drafts.

  • Condition, flaws and measurements up front
  • Shipping worked out by box size

How it works

From listing to paid in four steps.

  1. List it

    Photos, a price, and how you'll hand it over: pickup, drop-off or shipping.

  2. Buyer pays

    By card, held by Trove until delivery. Or they reserve it and pay you at the meetup.

  3. Hand it over

    Meet and enter their 6-digit code, or ship it with tracking.

  4. Get paid

    The money is released to you in full. No fee comes out of it.

Questions

Common questions

Is it really free?

Trove charges no fees to list, sell or buy. Cash and e-Transfer are free. Card and PayPal payments have a small processing fee (about 2.9% + 30¢, an estimate) that comes out of the sale.

What if an item doesn't arrive?

Card payments are held until delivery. If a seller doesn't ship by the deadline, the buyer is refunded automatically. If something arrives not as described, the buyer reports it before the money is released.

Can I pay in cash?

Yes, for meetups. Reserve the item and pay the seller in person. Paying in person isn't covered by the money-held protection.

What can I sell?

Almost anything legal: antiques, collectibles, home goods, clothing, electronics and more. Each listing shows its condition and any flaws up front.

Where does Trove work?

Trove starts in Canada, with prices in Canadian dollars. Shipping works across Canada, and meetups work wherever the buyer and seller are.

Is there a Chinese version?

Yes. Switch the whole site between English and Chinese from the menu.
